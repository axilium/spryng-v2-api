## Introduction

Email to SMS is a way to send a text message by sending a regular email, we then convert the email and send the SMS to your chosen recipients. You can send from your normal email service so no need to login to our UI or configure our public APIs, follow our setup guide to start sending today.

## Set up

Before beginning to send emails as SMS messages we need some additional information from you, login to the Esendex Connect portal and navigate to the message setting section.

- Proceed to message settings section
- Select Email to SMS tab
- Enter the email addresses who will be sending emails
- Enter any applicable IP addresses which will sending emails [optional]
- Select create new token
- Create an optional expiry date for the token
- Embed the generated token into your email [format below]

This token is unique for your account and should be kept secure, the token can reused for multiple emails / devices and multiple tokens can be generated.

You can now begin sending emails to us, send them to outbound@email2sms.spryng.nl along with the recipients who will receive the SMS, separated by a comma.

## Email body

The body of the email sent to us must follow the below format in order to successfully send an SMS to the intended recipient. If we receive an email from a unverified email address, an incorrect token or the format of the email is different from above then the email will be rejected and the SMS will not be sent.

##Token: FF5aeKiEyfXZFzEZbRYNqQkjzhB0CG
##Message: your message
##Recipients: 07917910440, 07757758731
##End

Any text entered into the Subject field will be ignored, along with signatures, images and attachments. Only text from the ##Message: section of the email will be sent via SMS to the recipient. Up to 10,000 recipients can be included per email.

## Security

**SPF & DKIM**

We use a third party tool for security checks to help keep our domain and your customers safe, we recommend your email addresses have SPF & DKIM setup to help improve this.

SPF (Sender Policy Framework) is a DNS record that lists all the servers and services authorized to send email on behalf of your domain.

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) adds a tamper-proof digital signature to your emails. This proves the email's content hasn't been altered.

SPF & DKIM help us authenticate your email messages and ensure someone is not spoofing emails in order to send spam. To set this up you must have access to your domain's DNS settings. This is where you manage your domain, not where you host your email. You'll find these settings at your "domain registrar" or "DNS provider.

**IP Address**

In order to improve security for both you and us, you can add your IP addresses to help us authenticate your email messages. This is optional but recommended as it adds an extra layer of authentication.

An IP address (which stands for Internet Protocol address) is a unique numerical label assigned to every device connected to a computer network.
